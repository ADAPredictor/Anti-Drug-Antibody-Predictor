Immunogenicity - the development of anti-drug antibodies (ADA) against therapeutic proteins - drives reduced efficacy, altered pharmacokinetics, and adverse events. Existing tools address single axes (MHC binding or B-cell epitopes) in isolation, and none integrate sequence-derived T/B-cell epitope signals with antibody CDR/germline calibration and a large clinical ADA reference. We present ADA Predictor, a web platform on the immunopred engine that combines MHC-I/II epitope prediction, B-cell linear epitope consensus scoring, antibody CDR delimitation and germline V-gene alignment, multi-signal risk flags with capped uplift, and an independent NAb risk score; a 289-biologic reference library (IDC-DS-V1) provides clinical ADA/NAb rates for background comparison without participating in the sequence score. For infliximab (chimeric anti-TNF-alpha), ADA Predictor reported a sequence-intrinsic risk of 58.2% (High) versus a clinical ADA of 34.23%; for adalimumab (fully human), 14.4% (Low) against a clinical ADA of 36.67% - illustrating that clinical ADA incidence is driven by non-sequence factors (dosing, aggregation, assay) and cannot be inferred from the molecule alone, while CDR/germline calibration distinguished chimeric, humanized, and fully human lineages. ADA Predictor offers transparent, reproducible, sequence-intrinsic risk assessment that complements clinical immunogenicity studies; decoupling molecular from clinical determinants enables honest early-stage candidate screening.
